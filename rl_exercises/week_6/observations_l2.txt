Observations:
In these short LunarLander-v3 runs, ActorCritic-GAE had the best final mean return, PPO vanilla was second, and PPO enhanced was most conservative. The enhanced PPO run had lower variance than ActorCritic-GAE, but it also achieved a lower final return. This is consistent with the purpose of KL early stopping and gradient clipping: they reduce the chance of very large policy updates, but can slow improvement when the total training budget is small.

PPO should usually become more competitive with longer training because it makes multiple clipped mini-batch updates from each rollout. The current 12000-step run is enough to produce comparison plots, but it is still noisy for LunarLander.
